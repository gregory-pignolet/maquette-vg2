Grading line

Every fruit passes under our cameras’ eye.

Our latest-generation grading line, a major investment, sorts and grades our fruit one by one.

  1. 1

    Bin emptying

    Harvest bins arrive from the orchard and are emptied onto the line.

  2. 2

    Washing

    Apples are washed.

  3. 3

    Waxing

    Apples are waxed so they keep longer.

  4. 4

    Camera sorting

    Cameras inspect every fruit for size and visual defects. Non-compliant fruit is removed automatically.

  5. 5

    Sorting and tray packing

    Each fruit is sent to the right station according to its size and quality, for tray packing.

  6. 6

    Pallet wrapping

    Pallets are stretch-wrapped for safe transport to the customer.

What it means for you

  • Consistent sizingEach box holds fruit of the same size.
  • Consistent qualitySorting is done fruit by fruit, every time.
  • Longer-keeping applesWaxing extends their shelf life.
  • Made to orderYour orders are prepared to the size you need.

Our story

Four generations on the banks of the Rhône.

In Saint-Pierre-de-Mézoargues, on the Rhône’s alluvial plains, the soil is highly fertile and ideal for orchards. Ghislain Sabatier, a third-generation farmer, set up here in 1984. Fanny Sabatier, the fourth generation, became a partner in 2022.

  1. 1984Ghislain Sabatier sets up on his first orchard plots.
  2. 1991The packing shed is built in Saint-Pierre-de-Mézoargues.
  3. 1994Specialisation in apricot packing.
  4. 1998Apple packing replaces lettuce and the shed is extended.
  5. 2009The farm becomes EARL Les Vergers de la Garenne.
  6. 2017HVE level 3 certification, among the first in the region.
  7. 2022Fanny Sabatier, fourth generation, becomes a partner.
  8. 2023The packing shed is extended.
  9. 2025To be completed.
  10. 2027To be completed.

FAQ

Your questions answered.

Which fruit do you offer, and when?

Cherries (early May to mid-June), apricots (late May to early August), peaches (early June to late August), nectarines (late June to mid-July) and apples (late July to mid-October): 39 varieties in total. See our harvest calendar for each variety.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes. We ship across France and abroad: Germany, the UK, Benelux, Asia (including India and Singapore), the Near and Middle East (including the United Arab Emirates) and South America.

What packaging do you offer?

Wood, cardboard, plastic crates or bushels, in any size: packaging is adapted to your requirements.

What are your certifications?

Our farm has been HVE level 3 certified since 2017. Our cherries, apricots and apples are GlobalG.A.P. certified (registration no. CMi 033/488/1/09, GGN 4050373087197) and GRASP certified.

Is your fruit hand-picked?

Yes. Our experienced pickers carry out a first sort in the orchard, to guarantee superior quality.
